# Typography

- Choose fonts that suit the subject, audience, and visual tone. Give typography clear roles, such as a distinctive display face for headlines and a highly readable face for supporting copy, rather than using one font for everything by default.
- Keep the type system coherent: use a small, deliberate font pairing and consistent hierarchy. Check readability at the final video size and against the actual background; follow [Text legibility](visual-storytelling.md#text-legibility) for contrast guidance.
