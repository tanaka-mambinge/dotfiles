# Gradients, glow, and shaders

- Avoid gradients or glow effects that look arbitrary, muddy, or overdone. If the visual needs a gradient or glow, look up polished examples online first and use a restrained treatment that fits the subject and keeps the focal point clear.
- [Paper Shaders](https://shaders.paper.design/) is optional inspiration for shader effects such as image filters, logo animations, and animated backgrounds. Browse it when a moment calls for a distinctive effect.
- Treat references as inspiration, not a required package or runtime. For HyperFrames, read `/hyperframes-animation` and its HTML-in-canvas or shader guidance, then use the project's supported approach. Do not add a dependency just to reproduce a reference effect.
